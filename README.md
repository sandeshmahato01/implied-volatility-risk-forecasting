# Does Implied Volatility Improve Market Risk Forecasting?

## Overview

This research project investigates whether **implied volatility provides useful additional information for forecasting financial market risk**.

The analysis was conducted in **R** as part of my research in finance, volatility modelling and market risk.

## Research Question

**Does implied volatility improve market risk forecasting compared with models based primarily on historical market information?**

## Research Focus

The project focuses on:

- Implied volatility
- Financial market volatility
- Market risk
- Value-at-Risk (VaR)
- Volatility forecasting
- Financial econometrics
- Risk-model evaluation

## Motivation

Traditional risk models rely heavily on historical market information.

Implied volatility, however, is derived from option prices and reflects market expectations about future uncertainty.

This research examines whether that forward-looking information can improve the forecasting of market risk.

## Tools

The analysis was conducted primarily in:

- R
- Financial time-series analysis
- Econometric modelling
- Risk modelling
- Data analysis and visualisation
